Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery typically takes 15-30 minutes depending on your location on campus. Real-time tracking available.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept cash, UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and campus wallets for maximum convenience.

Can I modify my order after placing it? +

Orders can be modified within 5 minutes of placement. Contact support for urgent changes.

Is there a minimum order value? +

No minimum order value! Order as little or as much as you want.

How do I report an issue with my order? +

Contact our support team via chat, email, or phone within 24 hours of delivery for immediate assistance.

How It Works

Step 1: Select Your Location −

Start by choosing your block and class number from our location selector in the navbar. This helps us deliver to the right place on campus.

Step 2: Browse & Order +

Explore our partner restaurants, select your favorite items, and add them to your cart. You can choose from pizza, burgers, shakes, snacks, and more!

Step 3: Checkout +

Review your cart, confirm the total amount, and proceed to payment. We accept cash, UPI, and all major payment methods.

Step 4: Track & Receive +

Once confirmed, your order will be prepared and delivered to your location. You'll receive your delicious food within 15-30 minutes!

Cancellation Policy

1. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled within 5 minutes of placement without any charges. After 5 minutes, if the order has been accepted by the restaurant, a cancellation fee may apply.

2. How to Cancel

To cancel an order, navigate to your active orders section and click the cancel button. You will receive a confirmation notification once the cancellation is processed.

3. Refund Processing

Refunds for cancelled orders will be processed within 3-5 business days back to your original payment method.

4. Preparation Started

If food preparation has already started, we may not be able to cancel your order. In such cases, the order will proceed, and you can request a refund after delivery.

5. Delivery Issues

If your order is not delivered within the estimated time, you can contact our support team for assistance or to request cancellation.

Refund Policy

1. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if your order is not delivered, is significantly delayed, or if the quality of the food does not meet our standards.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our customer support team within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence if applicable. We will investigate and process your request promptly.

3. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed within 3-5 business days back to your original payment method. In some cases, it may take longer depending on your bank.

4. Partial Refunds

In cases where only part of your order is affected, we will issue a partial refund for the affected items.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Delivery fees may not be refundable in all cases. Our support team will determine refund eligibility on a case-by-case basis.
